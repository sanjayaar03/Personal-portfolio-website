A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is this suitable for my business?+

I work with small and medium business owners who want a practical approach to attracting and following up with customers. The consultation helps us assess your goals and fit.

What happens during the free consultation?+

Let’s review your business, identify opportunities, and create a customized digital marketing plan you can start implementing immediately.

How much advertising budget do I need?+

There is no single budget that suits everyone. We consider your offer, market, goals, and ability to test. Advertising spend is separate from service fees.

How soon might I see results?+

Outcomes and timelines depend on your business, offer, budget, market, and starting point. We agree useful milestones and review progress without promising guaranteed results.

How do I get started?+

Request a free consultation and tell me a little about your business. I will respond to discuss the next step. A request does not confirm an appointment.
